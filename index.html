Tracking Lab

  2. Select a simulation mode if you'd like to simulate the experiment using a previously identified system.
  3. Click in the white area to the right to begin the experiment.
  4. Track the black line vertically with your mouse.
  5. Click in the white area again to stop the experiment.
  6. Click “Run System Identification” to analyze the data.
